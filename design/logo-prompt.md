# Aether logo — image-generation prompts (neon suite)

Tuned to match the existing rocket + checkmark icons: neon glow, hot-pink / electric-cyan /
yellow on a dark cosmic ground, soft bloom, slightly rough stenciled silhouette. Adds the
CRT-scanline / "every other row knocked out" effect for the *aether* concept.

## Core style block (paste into any prompt)

> 80s synthwave neon icon, glowing tube-light effect with soft bloom and halo, hot magenta-pink
> (#ff2e9a) and electric cyan-blue (#16c2ff) with yellow accents, on a dark near-black cosmic
> background with subtle radial nebula glow, rough hand-stenciled silhouette, retro CRT scanlines
> running horizontally across the shape (alternating rows knocked out for an ethereal transmitted
> look), punk DIY energy, high contrast, centered, app-icon composition, 1:1.

**D — "A" fused with a star**
> A stenciled letter "A" with a glowing four-point star replacing its apex, [core style block].

**E — wordmark**
> The word "AETHER" in bold condensed 80s neon lettering, [core style block]. Pink-to-cyan

## Tips to match your suite
- Keep the background the same near-black with a faint colored nebula bloom (your icons have a
  pink/yellow lower-left glow and cyan upper-right).
- Ask for **"soft outer glow / bloom"** explicitly — that halo is the signature of the suite.
- Ask for **"slightly imperfect / hand-cut stencil edges"** — your shapes aren't geometrically
  perfect, and that roughness is part of the punk feel.
- For consistency, generate at 1024×1024 like the existing two.
